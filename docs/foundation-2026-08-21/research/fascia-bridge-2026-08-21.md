## 5. The seam check — where elephant and Fascia conflict, and the compat contracts

**Seam 1 — naming: "energy" / "temperature" will collide.** Elephant's v\* is *participation energy* (volume/presence, an ensemble activity scalar). If Fascia's inter-cell signal is also called "energy" but means arousal/activation/effort, the two docs will quietly blur into one wrong claim. **Compat contract:** both teams register the term with its formula. Elephant owns "room energy = projection on v\* (the generalized-eigenvector channel)"; Fascia namespaces its tissue quantity (`fascia.energy`) and — if it is genuinely the shared-channel quantity — adopts the eigenvector *definition*, not just the word. If the Fascia spec's energy is per-cell (activation), it maps to elephant's volume dial, a *fiber* quantity, and must not be aggregated as if it were tissue.

**Seam 2 — the personality confound, fleet-scale.** Every Fascia cell (model) will have a stable idiosyncratic offset — a personality fiber. Any tissue-level valence aggregate will therefore mostly measure *the mix of cells present*, not the tissue state: exactly the category error REG-1 caught (the instrument fused energy with valence and read a mood ring as a thermometer). Fleet roster changes will masquerade as tissue events. **Compat contract:** import the dual-annotation discipline wholesale — every valence-ish Fascia output carries cos(valence-axis, tissue-channel) and cos(valence-axis, cell-personality-PC1); expect the first ≈ small, the second ≈ large, and treat roster-change-correlated jumps in valence aggregates as composition artifacts pending the ĉ-vs-residual decomposition (Gift 3's q). The κ-verdict sharpens this: entry re-aims the mean AND loosens concentration — a valence jump at model-entry is composition until decomposed.

**Seam 3 — the q-rule vs. Fascia's connective tissue (the real tension).** The q-rule descends from array processing that *rejects* the common mode as artifact; Fascia's whole thesis is that the common mode IS the tissue. These are not opposed — q certifies how much apparent common motion is genuinely collective vs pushed by shared artifact, which is *exactly* the honesty guarantee Fascia's tissue needs — but two operational conflicts remain: **(a) sealing order** (Ask 2: q needs the roster mean, the chain seals before it exists — resolve as seal-first/fuse-second, never feed the mean back); **(b) mutable fibers** — elephant's q denominates on *stable* per-cell offsets (ICC 0.77–0.91), but Fascia cells (models) may drift their fiber over time, breaking the denominator. **Compat contract:** define q_fascia on frozen-window offsets (per-session fiber estimate sealed at session start), or re-estimate the fiber on a slower clock than the tissue signal and log both clocks. And keep elephant's inversion in the spec text: this JEPA is trained to predict the tissue and *explicitly not* the personality variance — that's the load-bearing novelty claim, and it's the part standard JEPA has never done.

**Standing rule for both teams:** when in doubt, the registered-test discipline travels across the seam — anything either side claims about the school (emergence, gain, energy) gets a pre-stated null, a floor, and a branch verdict before it's rendered in a visualizer. A pixel without a null is a mood ring.

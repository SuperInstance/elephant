# MMX Multimodal Interpretations — 2026-08-21

The three initial visuals (mmx, cloud) were sent to MMX's VLM for cold-eyed
interpretation FOR THE TEAM. Verdicts: all three are "vision diagrams" (soul)
not "operational diagrams" (spec). Key takeaways:

## riverbed_001 (vMF field on hypersphere)
- Reads WELL as math: bright nodes = mean directions μ, tight glow = high κ,
  blue webbing = low κ / directional uncertainty; gold ribbons = premise
  grooves / high-probability paths; boundary fuzz = field breakdown into noise.
- Critiques: NO vector directionality (vMF is directional — this is scalar);
  ledger reads as "hairball" (no temporal order); 2D projection of S³ misleads
  about S⁶ antipodes; bands look biological, not geodesic.
- Asks: overlay arrows, color-code time (deep blue → gold), add equator/
  meridian grounding, make premise paths geodesics.

## tidegate_001 (three-state hysteresis automaton)
- Reads as steampunk mood board: CLEAR/IN/KILL chambers, brass manifold
  implies state linkage (hysteresis intuition correct).
- Critiques: no directionality/arrows, greeble numbers ("3.3.4", "66.%") with
  no meaning, static (hysteresis is a PATH, this shows no path), no water
  entry/exit, brass fittings don't connect — AI hallucination invites
  false-meaning hunting.
- Verdict: feeling of complexity without rules. Cannot build a state machine
  from it.

## ledger-river_001 (append-only ledger as river)
- Reads as vision diagram: complexity, permanence, collectivity.
- Critiques: NO attribution (who owns which branch), NO categorization
  (security patch vs rewrite indistinguishable), NO gates (where are review/
  tests? a river has no dams — a ledger must), NO scale markers (hour vs
  decade?), HEAD lost in the glowing horizon (can't find current state),
  uniform branch thickness (magnitude invisible).
- Verdict: "the soul without the operations" — fails as operational diagram.

## Conclusion for the team
The visuals succeed at AHA (they make the shape of the situation feelable) and
fail at SPEC (they don't encode the structure). That is the right division of
labor: diffusion/mmx renders the curvature of the situation; the team's
formalism provides the labeled overlay. Next iteration should render WITH
vector arrows, temporal gradients, and gate symbols — the local GPU forge
(sdxl-turbo on RTX 4050) is now online for fast iteration of these.
